# Workshop on Molecular Simulations of Biophysics and Biochemistry Nov. 21 (Thu.), 2013, RIKEN AICS

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |

## General Information

After [ICMS2013](http://web.apollon.nta.co.jp/icms2013/), we would like to hold a one-day workshop on molecular simulations on biophysics and biochemistry. Here we invite 7 professors (all of them are the invited speakers of ICMS2013) and 6 young researchers in Japan. At workshop, we hope to have fruitful discussion about both simulation applications and methodological developments in this field.
